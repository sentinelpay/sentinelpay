Straight answers

Questions people ask
before they get in touch

A screening layer that sits in front of your crypto business. Every wallet and every transaction is checked against sanctions lists, known bad actors and the patterns money launderers actually use, and you get a risk score with the reasons written out. It is a tool your compliance team runs, not a service that decides for you.

Exchanges, payment processors, OTC desks, custodians and any business that takes crypto and has to answer for where it came from. It is built for the small and mid sized ones, the teams of two or five who carry the same obligations as a bank without a bank's compliance department. We do not onboard gambling operators.

Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, BNB Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base and Tron, from one integration, with more added as our customers need them. If the chain you settle on is not on that list, tell us which one it is and we will say honestly whether it is close or far off.

You get an alert with a score and the named reasons behind it, before the funds settle rather than in a report a month later. What happens next is your decision and your policy: hold it, ask the customer, or let it through. Every check and every choice is logged, so when a regulator or a bank asks what you knew and when, you can show them.

We have not published prices yet, and we would rather say that than invent a number. Plans are shaped around how much you actually screen, so tell us your volume and we will tell you where you land. The trial is free and does not need a card.

Personal data is encrypted before it leaves our server, your email address is stored as a keyed hash so it is not sitting in a table in readable form, and nothing personal is written to a log we cannot delete from. Only our own team can reach it, through a separate secret, and we never use it for advertising. The whole of it, including how long we keep what and who processes it, is in our Privacy policy, in plain words.
