privacy policy

last updated: august 2026

this policy explains, in plain terms, what personal data sentinelpay collects, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it. we wrote it to be read, not filed away.

the short version

three questions get asked more than the rest, so they are answered here before the detail. everything below expands on these, and nothing below contradicts them.

is my data safethe personal fields are encrypted before they leave our server with aes-256-gcm, so a copy of the database on its own does not reveal them. your email address is additionally stored as a keyed hash, which lets us find your records without the address itself sitting in a table. passwords are never stored, only a slow one-way hash of them. nothing that identifies you is written to a log we cannot delete from.
where is it keptin a postgres database run by our hosting provider, reached only over an encrypted connection. the providers that touch personal data on our behalf are named in who we share it with, and each is bound by a data processing agreement.
who can see itonly the sentinelpay team, through an interface that requires a separate secret. it is not connected to the product, it is not shared with customers, and it is never used for advertising. we do not sell personal data.

how long each kind of data is kept, and what deletes it, is in how long we keep it. to have your data erased, email privacy@sentinelpay.org; we answer within one month.

who we are

sentinelpay is a crypto risk and compliance company. we provide a monitoring platform that helps crypto businesses screen wallets and transactions, detect exposure to financial crime, and decide whether to accept a payment before funds are released.

sentinelpay is the "data controller" for the personal data described in this policy, which means we decide how and why it is processed. our full legal entity name, company registration number and registered office will be published here before we launch commercially. in the meantime, all the ways to reach us, including live chat, are set out in how to reach us at the end of this policy.

the data we collect

depending on how you use sentinelpay, we may collect, use, store and transfer the following kinds of personal data:

identity datafirst name, last name, job title, and, where we are legally required to verify you, identification documents and similar information.
contact dataemail address, company name, telephone number and billing address.
payment databilling details and the crypto wallet addresses used to pay for our services. we do not store full card numbers; card payments, if offered, are handled by a regulated payment processor.
crypto activity datawallet addresses, transaction identifiers, on-chain history, sanctions and watchlist matches, mixer and darknet exposure, and other signals we use to identify illicit activity and produce a risk score. most of this originates from public blockchains.
technical dataip address, browser type and version, time zone and location, operating system and platform, device identifiers and other technology on the devices you use to reach our site and services.
usage datainformation about how you log into, navigate and use our site and product.
communications & marketing datathe content of your messages to us, your support history, and your preferences for receiving updates and marketing.

we do not intentionally collect special category data (such as health, religion or biometric data) in the ordinary course of providing the service. where crypto activity data reveals a person's association with suspected criminal activity, we handle it under the specific legal condition described further below.

how we collect it

  • directly from you, when you fill in a form, join our newsletter or waitlist, apply to become a customer, or contact us.
  • automatically, through cookies and similar technologies when you use our website.
  • when you connect a public key and use the product, so we can scan the wallets and transactions that touch your business.
  • from public and licensed sources, such as blockchains, sanctions lists and threat intelligence, when we build and maintain our risk database.
  • from interactions such as email, phone or video calls. calls may be recorded, and you will be told before any recording begins.

how we use it, and our legal basis

we only process personal data where the law gives us a basis to do so: because you have consented, because it is necessary to perform a contract with you, because we have a legal obligation, or because we have a legitimate interest that does not override your rights.

where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object, and we will stop unless we have compelling grounds to continue.

automated processing and ai

sentinelpay uses automated analysis, including machine learning, to score wallets and flag risk. this is central to what the product does. it is not "solely automated decision-making" with legal or similarly significant effects on an individual: our system produces a risk score and context, and the decision to accept, hold or decline a transaction always remains with the human operator using the product. where an ai feature would process your voice, image or free-text inputs, we will ask for your consent first.

how we keep it safe

we host and process personal data on reputable cloud infrastructure and protect it with layered safeguards: encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, least-privilege access so only staff who need data can reach it, network protections, logging and monitoring. no system is perfectly secure, but we work to industry-standard practices and review them as we grow.

if we ever suffer a personal data breach that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the relevant regulator and, where required, affected individuals, without undue delay.

who we share it with

  • our customers, who receive the crypto activity data and risk analysis they are entitled to as part of the product.
  • trusted service providers (for hosting, analytics, communications, payments and support), bound by data processing agreements to protect your data to at least our standard and to use it only on our instructions.
  • authorities, where we are legally required to disclose data to a court, regulator or law enforcement, or where disclosure helps prevent serious unlawful activity.
  • professional advisers and potential acquirers, in connection with legal advice or a corporate transaction, under appropriate confidentiality.

the providers that touch personal data on our behalf, and what each of them is for:

railwayhosting. it runs our servers and holds the database and the server logs.
cloudflarethe network in front of the site. it sees the ip address of every request and runs the check that tells a person from a bot.
resendemail delivery. it handles the messages we send you, such as a verification code or a reply to your enquiry.
intercomthe live chat, if you open it.

each is bound by a data processing agreement to use your data only on our instructions and to protect it to at least our standard. we do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

international transfers

we may transfer personal data to countries outside your own, including to service providers located elsewhere. where we do, we use appropriate safeguards recognised under applicable data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses, so your data keeps a comparable level of protection.

how long we keep it

we keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, plus any period required by law. after that we anonymise it (if we still want it for analytics), pseudonymise it (if we may need it again for a legitimate purpose), or delete it. crypto activity data is an exception: because the integrity of our risk analysis depends on historical context, we may retain it for as long as necessary to provide our services.

the periods we hold ourselves to, and none of them depend on anyone remembering: form submissions, 365 days. accounts, until 24 months with no sign-in. server logs, 30 days. a job runs every day and deletes whatever is past its date.

what happens to a form you send us

when you fill in the demo or free trial form, we store what you typed: your name, work email, job title, company name and website, and the industry and country you selected. we also store the ip address the request came from, the browser you used and the time, because without those we cannot tell a genuine enquiry from an automated one.

we keep it to answer you, to decide whether to open a trial account, and to be able to show later what we checked and when. that is our legitimate interest in running and protecting the service.

where it is keptin a database, not in email. the personal fields are encrypted before they leave our server, so a copy of the database on its own does not reveal them. your email address is additionally stored as a keyed hash, which lets us find your records without the address itself sitting in the table.
how long365 days from the day you send it. a job runs daily and deletes anything past that, automatically, whether or not anyone remembers to.
who can reach itonly the sentinelpay team, through an interface that requires a separate secret. it is not connected to the product, it is not shared with customers, and it is never used for advertising.
what our servers write downone line per submission, with nothing personal in it: which form, whether it was accepted, the country and a short reference. your name, your address and your ip address are not in that line. it goes to our hosting provider's log store, which we cannot delete a single line out of, so nothing that identifies you is put there in the first place. those logs are kept 30 days.
deleting it sooneremail privacy@sentinelpay.org and ask. we remove every record tied to your address, from the database and from the backup file alike, and we do not need you to prove anything beyond controlling that mailbox. you do not have to explain why.

if you create an account

an account holds your name, your work email and your password. the password is never stored: what we keep is a slow one-way hash of it, which can check a password you type but cannot be turned back into one. your email address is encrypted, and stored a second time as a keyed hash so we can find your account without the address itself sitting in the table.

before an account exists we email a six digit code to the address you gave and wait for you to type it back. that is how we know the address is really yours. the code is not stored either, only a hash of it, and it is deleted the moment it is used, expires, or is guessed wrong too many times. if you never finish, the half-made sign-up deletes itself within a day.

how longfor as long as you use the account. after 24 months with no sign-in we delete it, automatically, on the same daily job that clears everything else.
deleting it soonerthe same address as above. asking us to erase your data removes the account and anything half-made under the same address.

your rights

depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and check we are processing it lawfully.
  • correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • ask us to delete your data where we have no overriding reason to keep it.
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.
  • ask us to restrict processing, for example while its accuracy is checked.
  • receive your data in a portable format, or ask us to transfer it.
  • withdraw consent where you previously gave it.

to exercise any of these, email privacy@sentinelpay.org. we may first need to verify your identity, so that personal data is never disclosed to the wrong person. we answer within one month of receiving your request, and we write back to confirm once it is done. if a request is unusually complex we may need up to two months more, and we will tell you inside the first month if that happens. these rights are free to exercise in normal circumstances.

cookies

our website uses cookies, small text files stored on your device, in two main ways:

  • essential cookies, which make the site work, remember your preferences, and facilitate secure login to private areas.
  • analytics cookies, which tell us in aggregate how the site is used (browser, operating system, pages viewed, paths taken, approximate location from ip) so we can improve it.

most browsers let you delete or block cookies through their settings. disabling essential cookies may stop parts of the site from working.

children

sentinelpay is a business-to-business product and is not directed at children. we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. if you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

other sites

our site and product may link to third-party websites and services we do not control. this policy does not cover them, and we encourage you to read their own privacy notices.

our crypto activity database

sentinelpay maintains a database built largely from publicly available cryptocurrency data, such as wallet addresses, transaction ledgers and blockchains. we combine this with information about a wallet's association with known or suspected criminal activity and with publicly available sanctions lists.

some of that information relates to a person's association with suspected criminal activity, which in some jurisdictions is treated as a special category of data. where that applies, we process it under a substantial public interest condition, such as the prevention or detection of unlawful acts. sentinelpay is the data controller for the personal data in this database.

customers may submit crypto information to us, such as wallet addresses or transaction hashes. we combine it with our database to assess how likely the activity is to involve crime or sanctioned parties, and we return that risk level to the customer, who decides how to proceed. where a high risk is identified, a customer may decline a transaction or request more information, and in serious cases relevant information may be shared with authorities working to prevent crime.

how to reach us

pick the lane that fits. a real person is on the other end, and we would rather answer early than have you guess.

sales, product & supportquestions about the product, pricing, onboarding or your account? open a live chat from the button in the bottom-right corner, or email support@sentinelpay.org. live chat is usually the fastest way to reach us.
complaintsif you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. we would genuinely appreciate the chance to put it right first.

changes to this policy

we may update this policy as our product and the law evolve. where a change is significant, we will make reasonable efforts to tell customers in advance, but please check back from time to time. the date at the top always shows when it was last updated.
