we'll take almost any crypto business. gambling is where we draw the line

ceem, mind, chibbyjuly 28, 2026 · 3 min read

i want to say this plainly, before anyone has to ask. sentinelpay is built for crypto businesses of almost every shape. gambling is the one thing we will never touch, and i am not going to be diplomatic about why. we think it is one of the most destructive businesses on earth, and we refuse to be any part of it.

first, the part that is wide open. if you take crypto for a real product or a real service, we want to work with you. payment processors. otc desks. small exchanges. freelancers getting paid in usdt. the shop on the corner that started accepting crypto last month. you all carry the same core problem, a wallet can be dirty no matter who it pays, and we built sentinelpay so you can finally see it before it costs you. that door is open, and it stays open.

and then there is gambling

online casinos, sportsbooks, betting platforms, the whole category. we will not onboard them, ever, and i would rather be blunt about the reason than hide behind a tidy policy line.

gambling is not a normal business that happens to carry some risk. it is a machine engineered to take money from people who cannot afford to lose it, and to keep them coming back long after they already have. the loss is not a side effect, the loss is the product. the house is built to win, everyone running it knows the house is built to win, and every dollar of profit comes straight out of someone getting hurt. the crypto version just stripped away the few limits the old one was forced to keep: no closing time, no cooling-off, no borders, anonymous, instant, and sitting in your pocket at three in the morning.

and on top of all that, it is one of the places money laundering concentrates, because a casino is almost purpose-built to take dirty money in, mix it, and pay it back out looking clean. so it is both morally rotten and a compliance nightmare at the same time. for us that is not a hard call. it is the easiest no we will ever make.

we are not going to build the layer that makes a machine like that look respectable. helping it pass as legitimate is worse than doing nothing at all.

what we actually stand for

we started sentinelpay to protect the business that gets used, the honest operator who takes a bad payment and pays for it months later. gambling sits on the exact opposite side of that. one side gets hurt without meaning to. the other side does the hurting on purpose, for money, at scale. we know which side we want to stand on, and it is not a close question.

and yes, i know exactly what this costs us. gambling operators have enormous volume and they pay extremely well. we are turning every bit of it away, on purpose, permanently. i would rather run a smaller company i can be proud of than get rich helping the most predatory business in crypto look clean.

where the line actually is

so there is no confusion, here is the exact edge. we work with businesses that accept crypto for goods, services, trading, payments, and payroll. we do not work with online casinos, sportsbooks, betting or wagering platforms, or anything whose core product is gambling. if you are genuinely not sure which side you land on, just ask us, and we will tell you straight.

bottom line

every company claims to have values right up until the values cost real money. this one costs us a fortune we will never see, and we are keeping it without a second thought. sentinelpay is for the people building something real. it will never be for the house, no matter what they put on the table.

if that is the kind of company you want watching your back, follow along and get early access when we launch at sentinelpay.org.
